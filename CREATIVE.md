# Creative decision

The poster is a dark navy editorial poster. A deep navy field anchors the whole composition, with cyan and violet glows that spread from a central neural-net node motif — thin glowing lines weaving between glowing dots to suggest an intelligence map. Rising candlesticks (green/red, clean but luminous) chart upward along the right side, tying the crypto/market theme to the "AI agents trading on insight" idea. The headline `@crisxai5` is set in large, legible white-on-navy serif, backlit by a soft cyan glow so it reads at a glance. A thin grid in the background adds a data-ledger texture. The subtitle `AI AGENTS  /  CRYPTO INSIGHTS  /  WEB3` sits at the bottom in small caps with a cyan underline, exactly as the brief specifies, and the whole piece stays legible on a phone poster thumbnail.
